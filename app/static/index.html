Event status

StatusMeaning
PENDINGNot delivered yet. Either no attempt has been made, or attempts failed but some are left. It will be tried again on Deliver or Process pending.
SUCCEEDEDAn attempt succeeded. Done, not sent again.
FAILEDAll 5 attempts failed. Final, not tried again.

A failed attempt (outcome FAILURE) does not make the event FAILED; it stays PENDING until an attempt succeeds or all 5 are used.
